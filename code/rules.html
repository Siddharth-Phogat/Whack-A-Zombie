Game Rules

Zombies

Click on zombies to score points. Earn 10 points for each zombie you eliminate.

Humans

Avoid clicking on humans or you will lose a life. You start with 3 lives.

Jumpscares

Beware of random jumpscares that may appear during gameplay!

Score

Track your score in real time. Score 500 to win!

Time

You have 60 seconds to score as many points as possible. The game ends when time runs out.

Lives

You have 3 lives. Lose all 3 and the game is over.

Toggle-Gun

Enable the "Gun" to add extra atmosphere in the game.

Dark-Mode

If normal Gameplay is easy, try beating the game with limited visibility and fog effects.

Toggle-Flicker

Use the "Flicker Mode" button to toggle flickering effects on tiles.
